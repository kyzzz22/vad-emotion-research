# DREAMER access and local placement

Official access page: https://zenodo.org/records/546113

The record is public, but the dataset files are restricted. Log in to Zenodo,
select **Request access**, and submit the following text after replacing the
bracketed fields.

## Request text

```text
Name: [Your full name]
Institution: [Your university or research institution]
Position: [Master's student / PhD student / Researcher]
Contact email: [Your institutional email]

I am requesting access to the DREAMER database for non-commercial academic
research on the relationship between lexical affect norms and stimulus-elicited
emotional experience. Specifically, I plan to compare NRC-VAD word ratings with
participants' self-reported valence, arousal, and dominance after audiovisual
emotion elicitation, and to examine whether EEG and ECG features explain the
discrepancy between lexical and experienced VAD coordinates.

The data will be used only by our research team, stored securely, not
redistributed, and cited in accordance with the DREAMER dataset paper and its
terms of access.
```

## After approval

Download the official archive with your own Zenodo account and extract it in
this directory. Do not place account credentials in this project and do not
redistribute the raw dataset.

Expected main file in common DREAMER releases:

```text
public_data/licensed/dreamer/DREAMER.mat
```

The exact approved archive structure will be inspected before the analysis is
run. The CASE pilot remains in `public_data/case_pilot/` and will not be
overwritten.
